# Why a blog?
The human need to share ones experience. No matter the importance.
Let's discuss the question why I'm choosing to write a blog instead of creating videos.
Compared to a creating video, writing a blog has a much lower barrier of entry. This will make it easier for me to give regular updates. Creating a Video is something I fancy, however, I don't know how to do. Especially, producing it in such a way that I'd feel comfortable publishing is something that would take a substantial amount of time. When life gets busy in China this will result in doing nothing. Thus, writing a blog is the way to go! (Hopefully)
I could also imagine adding some form of email list, in such a way that people interested get notified for new blog entries.
